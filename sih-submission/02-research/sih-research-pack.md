# SIH Research Pack

## 1. Problem Analysis
Current physical verification processes lead to manual data entry errors, high pendency, and difficulty in tracking the entire lifecycle (initial stamping to re-verification) of instruments.

## 2. Regulatory Analysis
- **Legal Metrology Act, 2009:** Mandates standards of weights and measures. Section 24 covers verification and stamping.
- **GATC Rules, 2013:** Allows private entities (Government Approved Test Centres) to verify specific weights/measures, necessitating a system that supports both LMOs (officials) and GATCs (private entities) under a unified controller.

## 3. Existing System Analysis
Many states have localized portals (e.g., e-Maaptaal) that act as simple form-submission tools. They often lack a unified, instrument-centric lifecycle view, live QR authentication, and robust offline field apps.

## 4. Gap Analysis
**Current:** Fragmented state-level document tracking.
**Proposed:** Unified, instrument-centric digital twin with live public auditability and rule-engine abstraction.

## 5. Innovation Analysis
The primary innovation is the **Instrument-Centric Architecture combined with a Configurable Rule Engine**. Instead of hardcoding rules, the system allows the Controller to define parameters (fees, validity periods) ensuring the platform scales nationally without code changes.
